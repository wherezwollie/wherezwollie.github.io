## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Hero Section with Instant Solar Estimator Widget**:
   - Interactive bill selector button group ($300-$500, $500-$900, $900-$1500, $1500+).
   - Real-time updates for recommended kW system size, annual savings, and payback period.

2. **About Me / Local Specialist Bio**:
   - Highlighting local Gold Coast CEC credentials, Queensland Electrical License (#88412), marine-grade coastal roof fittings, and direct electrician contact.

3. **Solar Brand Showcase (With Category Filters)**:
   - **Inverters & Microinverters**: Fronius (Austria 🇦🇹), Enphase IQ8 Microinverters (USA 🇺🇸), Sungrow Hybrid (Global #1 🌏).
   - **Solar Panels**: REC Alpha Pure-R HJT (Norway/Singapore 🇳🇴), LONGi Hi-MO X6 Ultra (Tier 1 🏆).
   - **Battery Storage**: Tesla Powerwall 3 (13.5 kWh Whole-Home Backup 🇺🇸).
   - Interactive filter tabs for equipment category selection.

4. **Interactive Gold Coast Solar Savings Calculator**:
   - Sliders for average quarterly power bill & daytime energy usage %.
   - Toggle checkbox for solar battery storage.
   - Calculates annual bill savings, recommended solar array size, payback years, 25-year total savings, and CO2 offset.

5. **Multi-Step "Get a Quote" Form Wizard**:
   - Step 1: Property Type & Suburb.
   - Step 2: System Goals (New Solar, Battery, Upgrade, EV Charger).
   - Step 3: Preferred Solar Brands.
   - Step 4: Contact Info (Name, Phone, Email, Address, Notes).
   - Interactive modal submission popup with quote summary.

6. **Contact Details & Local Service Suburbs**:
   - Phone `(07) 5500 0000`, Email, Bundall depot address, business hours, and interactive Gold Coast suburb chips (Burleigh, Robina, Surfers, Southport, Coomera, Tweed, etc.).

7. **Hosting Test & Diagnostics Widget**:
   - Displays live host domain/protocol, DOM load speed (ms), asset bundling health, and viewport dimensions.
