Why work directly with a local Gold Coast master electrician?

For over 15 years, I've lived and worked right here on the Gold Coast—from Tweed Heads up to Ormeau and across the Hinterland. Unlike national sub-contracting call centers that sell cheap pre-packaged solar kits, I handle your system design and installation personally.
